Troubleshooting

Common Issue Diagnostics

Device Overheating Issues

If your device feels hot to the touch during simple tasks, it is usually caused by active background software loops, a degraded lithium battery running under elevated load resistance, or processor throttling from CPU load.

Suggested Action: Battery Check / CPU diagnostics Average Fix Time: 20-30 Mins
Rapid Battery Drain

Batteries naturally degrade after 500 charge cycles. If you see battery percentage dropping rapidly, our technicians can measure battery health and performance peaks. A physical battery replacement restores peak factory capacity instantly.

Suggested Action: Battery Replacement Average Fix Time: 15-20 Mins
Black Screen of Death / Unresponsive Display

An unresponsive black screen can be triggered by a severed display ribbon flex connector (following drop impacts), back-light circuit failures on the board, or a deep software freeze requiring a hardware reset logic cycle.

Suggested Action: Display Replacement / Digitizer reseat Average Fix Time: 30-40 Mins
Charging Failure / Loose Cable Connection

If the device charges intermittently or only when the cable is angled, dust/lint blockage inside the port is likely. If lint cleaning fails, the metal contact traces in the port have worn out, requiring a new charging port connector logic strip.

Suggested Action: Port Cleansing / Charging Port Swap Average Fix Time: 20-30 Mins
Extreme Operating System Lag & Freezes

System stuttering is typically linked to corrupted firmware partitions, system caches being full, or failing onboard storage chips. A complete software flash restores native responsive velocities.

Suggested Action: Custom OS flash / partition reset Average Fix Time: 45-60 Mins
Touchscreen ghost clicks / dead areas

Ghost clicks or non-responsive dead zones across the screen represent a crack in the capacitive digitizer grid array. This requires replacing the glass digitizer assembly to restore clean touch registration.

Suggested Action: Digitizer Screen Swap Average Fix Time: 30 Mins
Infinite Boot Loop / Stuck on Apple/Android logo

Sticking on the startup screen is triggered by corrupted kernel software, power key circuits shorting out, or storage blocks failing. We inspect the logic board and flash firmware to recover normal boot cycles.

Suggested Action: Hardware Diagnostics / Flash restore Average Fix Time: 60-90 Mins

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful clarifications on refurbished devices, testing, trade-ins, and warranty terms.
What is the battery health percentage guaranteed on your refurbished devices?

We guarantee a minimum of 85% battery health capacity on all pre-owned smartphones and tablets. If health drops below that threshold during testing, our technicians swap it with a brand-new high-quality replacement battery prior to sale.

How does the 12-Month Store Warranty work?

Our store warranty covers diagnostics, parts, and hardware labour charges for digitizer failures, power IC issues, software freezes, and camera calibration anomalies. Note that physical drops, liquid submersion, or third-party disassembly void the warranty.

How is the online trade-in payout computed?

The online trade-in appraiser calculates a payout value based on device model, storage tier, carrier configuration, and wear condition. Once you lock in the quote, the value is frozen for 7 days. Drop by our counter to verify condition details and collect your cash.

What carrier configurations do you offer?

Almost 95% of our refurbished smartphones are fully factory unlocked, allowing you to insert SIM cards from T-Mobile, AT&T, Verizon, or international carriers immediately. Carrier-locked models are clearly marked with custom badges.

Can I cancel or reschedule my walk-in appointment?

Yes. You can reschedule or cancel at any time. Simply tap the link in the confirmation SMS or email, or call our branch phone number to secure a new time slot.
