Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our repairs, parts, warranties, and data safety.
How long does a typical repair take?

Most common repairs like screen and battery replacements take around 15 to 30 minutes. You can wait at our service center counter, grab a drink, or browse nearby stores while we finish.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes! We offer a lifetime warranty on parts and labor for screen and battery replacements. If touch accuracy degrades, simply visit our service center for a free replacement.

Will I lose my data during the repair?

No. We do not access or reset your personal data during screen, port, or battery repairs. However, we advise creating a backup prior to the repair for hardware safety.

Do you use original components?

We use original grade premium components (OEM specifications) which undergo strict quality testing to ensure identical color profile, refresh speed, and touch sensitivity.

Are your replacement parts genuine or aftermarket?

We use only premium OEM-grade components sourced from certified supply chains — never cheap aftermarket knockoffs. Every display is tested for color accuracy and touch response, every battery for true mAh capacity, and every flex cable for signal integrity before installation. Our parts carry a full warranty against defects.

Are your technicians certified and trained?

Absolutely. Every FixPoint technician holds industry-recognized IPC-7711/7721 SMD rework certification and Level-3 logic board diagnostics credentials. Our team averages 8+ years of hands-on experience and undergoes continuous OEM training to stay current with the latest device architectures, including Apple, Samsung, and Google Pixel platforms.
