What We Do

Board-Level Component Repair

Motherboard failures are often caused by microscopic component damage — a blown capacitor, a cracked solder joint, a shorted IC, or corroded trace lines. Unlike basic part swaps, these repairs require specialized equipment and advanced diagnostic skills.

At FixPoint, our technicians use Amscope trinocular microscopes, JBC precision soldering stations, and thermal imaging cameras to locate and repair faults at the component level. We specialize in power management IC replacement, baseband processor reballing, audio IC swaps, and NAND flash recovery.

Warning Signs

Symptoms Requiring Board Repair

If your device shows any of these symptoms, it likely needs motherboard-level diagnosis.

No Power / Dead

Phone won't turn on despite a charged battery and working charger.

Boot Loop

Device continuously restarts without reaching the home screen.

No Signal / IMEI

Lost cellular signal, "No Service" or missing IMEI number.

Overheating

Extreme heat from the board area, rapid battery drain, or thermal shutdowns.

How It Works

Micro-Soldering Process

Advanced board-level repair requires precision at every step.
01

Advanced Diagnostics

We perform multi-meter voltage injection, thermal imaging scans, and microscope inspection to identify the exact failed component — whether it's a shorted capacitor, lifted pad, or blown IC.

02

Component Sourcing

Replacement ICs, capacitors, resistors, and connectors are sourced from verified OEM-grade suppliers. For reballing, we use leaded or lead-free solder balls matching the original BGA footprint.

03

Precision Soldering

Using hot-air rework stations with temperature-controlled airflow, we remove the defective component, clean the pads, apply fresh flux, and solder the replacement under magnification.

04

Full Function Verification

After repair, we run comprehensive diagnostics: power delivery, cellular connectivity, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, sensors, charging, and a 2-hour burn-in stability test to ensure lasting reliability.

Common Questions

Motherboard Repair FAQs

Will I lose my data during motherboard repair?

Data preservation is our top priority for board-level repairs. In most cases, your data remains intact since we repair the board rather than replacing it. For NAND-related failures, we use specialized readers to extract data before repair.

How long does a micro-soldering repair take?

Most board-level repairs take 1 to 3 business days. Simple component swaps like audio IC or charging IC can sometimes be completed same-day. Complex repairs involving NAND recovery or multiple component failures may take up to 5 days.

What if the repair isn't successful?

We operate a strict "no fix, no fee" policy. If we're unable to repair your device's motherboard, you only pay the diagnostic fee ($39). We'll provide a detailed report of the damage and discuss alternative options with you.

Is micro-soldering safe for my phone?

When performed by trained technicians with proper equipment, micro-soldering is very safe. We use temperature-controlled hot-air stations, lead-free solder, and ESD-safe workstations. Every repair is performed under magnification to ensure precision.
