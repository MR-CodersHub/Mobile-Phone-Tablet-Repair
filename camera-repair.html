What We Do

Camera Module Replacement & Calibration

Modern phone cameras are精密 assemblies combining a lens stack, image sensor, OIS voice-coil motor, and flex cable — any of which can fail. We diagnose the exact cause (software vs. hardware) and repair or replace the affected camera module.

After installation, we calibrate autofocus, verify OIS response across the full range of motion, check for imaging artifacts, and ensure all lenses (wide, ultra-wide, telephoto) function correctly.

How It Works

Camera Repair Process

Diagnose, repair, calibrate, and certify.
01

Software & Hardware Diagnostics

We test each camera sensor individually, check OIS response across axis, and inspect the lens assembly under magnification for cracks, dust ingress, or moisture damage.

02

Camera Module Replacement

Failed camera modules are carefully desoldered or unseated from the motherboard. A new OEM-grade module is installed with proper EMI shielding and thermal interface material.

03

Calibration & Alignment

We run calibration routines for autofocus range, OIS travel limits, white balance reference, and sensor gain mapping. A multi-distance focus chart is used to verify sharpness.

04

Image Quality Certification

Test photos are taken in varying lighting conditions to check for focus accuracy, exposure consistency, color uniformity across lenses, and OIS effectiveness during hand shake.

Common Questions

Camera Repair FAQs

My camera shows a black screen — is it hardware or software?

A black viewfinder is usually a hardware issue with the camera module or its flex connection. We run a full diagnostic to rule out software causes (which are rare) before any repair work begins.

What is OIS and can it be repaired?

Optical Image Stabilization uses tiny voice-coil motors to shift the lens and counter hand shake. If the OIS is stuck, rattling, or making noise, the camera module typically needs replacement. We recalibrate the new module's OIS range during installation.

Will camera repair affect Face ID?

Face ID on iPhones uses the TrueDepth camera system (infrared projector, flood illuminator, and dot projector). Rear camera replacement does not affect Face ID. Front camera replacement may affect it on some models — we will advise you before proceeding.
