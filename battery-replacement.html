What We Do

Expert Battery Replacement

Lithium-ion batteries naturally degrade over time, losing capacity and causing unexpected shutdowns. At FixPoint, we test your battery's health (cycle count, peak current delivery, internal resistance) and replace it with a premium-grade cell meeting or exceeding OEM specifications.

Every installation includes Battery Management System calibration, adhesive resealing, and a full charge-cycle test to ensure proper current draw and temperature regulation.

Battery Health Assessment

Common Questions

Battery Repair FAQs

Why does my battery drain so fast?

Battery capacity naturally degrades with each charge cycle. After 500 cycles, most lithium-ion batteries retain only about 80% of their original capacity. A replacement restores full capacity and peak current delivery.

Will I lose my data during the repair?

No. Battery replacement does not affect your data, settings, or apps. We only disconnect and reconnect the battery — your storage remains untouched. However, we always recommend a backup before any repair.

Is aftermarket battery replacement safe?

Yes, when done with quality parts and proper procedures. We use only OEM-grade cells with built-in protection circuits. Our technicians follow strict safety protocols including ESD-safe tools and proper torque specifications.
