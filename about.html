Service Center History & Labs

Our Mission & Story

Meet the micro-soldering specialists and calibration engineers bringing next-generation device restoration directly to shopping centers.

Quality Diagnostics, Performed in Public View

FixPoint was launched with a single objective: to transform device diagnostics and chip-level logic board soldering into a public, premium service. We believe customer trust is won when the repair bench is open and transparent.

By positioning clean, high-tech glass workspaces in primary retail hubs, we eliminated the stress of shipping phones away. Today, we manage advanced regional micro-soldering labs, recover water-damaged assemblies, and recycle electronic waste, keeping over 15,000 devices active.

Operational Standards

Every diagnostic microscope workspace employs electrostatic discharge protection, multi-point calibration standards, and certified logic analyzers.

Corporate Timeline

2018: Ground Zero

Launched Service Center #1 at Tech City West Galleria Mall. Initial concept for public display repairs established.

2020: Regional Expansion

Opened 5 regional service center locations. Established internal diagnostic parts warehousing supply chains.

2023: Logic Board Lab

Opened in-house logic board micro-soldering cleanroom labs, supporting complex IC chip swaps.

2026: 15,000+ Fixed

Celebrated over 15,000 successful smartphone repairs, saving tons of micro-electronics from e-waste.

Our Values

Commitment Beyond Repair

We take responsibility for the devices we fix, the technicians we train, and the environment we operate in.

Eco-Conscious Operations

We recycle 100% of replaced batteries, shattered glass, and damaged circuit boards through certified e-waste partners. Our packaging uses only recycled materials.

Continuous Training

Every technician completes 200+ hours of lab training per year, covering new device architectures, micro-soldering techniques, and diagnostic software updates.

Community Support

We donate restored devices to local schools and non-profits. Each year, over 500 refurbished tablets are placed with students who lack access to technology.
