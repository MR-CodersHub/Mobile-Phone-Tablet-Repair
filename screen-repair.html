What We Do

Premium Display Replacement

A cracked screen is more than a cosmetic issue — it exposes your device to dust, moisture, and further internal damage. At FixPoint, we replace shattered glass, bleeding LCDs, and unresponsive digitizers with precision-calibrated OEM-grade panels.

Every replacement includes EEPROM data migration to restore True Tone, auto-brightness, and original color profiles. We use UV-curable LOCA adhesive and pre-cut thermal gaskets to match factory water resistance.

How It Works

Screen Repair Process

From drop-off to calibrated display, here is exactly what happens to your device.
01

Diagnostic Assessment

We inspect the crack pattern, digitizer functionality, and check for underlying LCD bleeding or touch dead zones. A full device function test is performed.

02

Panel Selection & Data Migration

We match your device with the correct OEM-grade panel. Using EEPROM programmers, we extract the original display calibration data and flash it onto the new screen controller.

03

Precision Installation

The old screen is carefully removed, the frame is cleaned of残余 adhesive, and the new panel is installed with UV-cured LOCA and pre-cut waterproof gaskets.

04

Common Questions

Screen Repair FAQs

Will I lose True Tone or Face ID after the repair?

No. We use specialized EEPROM programmers to migrate the original display calibration data from your broken screen to the new panel. True Tone, auto-brightness, and Face ID all remain fully functional.

How long does a screen replacement take?

Most screen replacements are completed in 20 minutes or less. Complex repairs like foldable displays or iPad screens may take 40-60 minutes. You are welcome to wait at our counter or browse nearby stores.

Is the screen quality the same as the original?

We use premium OEM-grade displays that match original specifications for color gamut, brightness, and touch sensitivity. Every panel undergoes pre-installation testing to verify pixel uniformity and digitizer response.
