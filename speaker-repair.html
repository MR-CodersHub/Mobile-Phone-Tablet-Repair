What We Do

Audio Component Diagnosis & Repair

Phone audio problems can stem from clogged speaker grilles, blown voice coils, damaged diaphragms, or corroded flex connectors. We use audio signal injection and impedance testing to pinpoint the exact failed component — then clean or replace it.

We service all audio pathways: bottom speaker (music/calls), earpiece receiver, microphones (primary and noise-canceling), and the ringer/vibrator assembly.

How It Works

Speaker Repair Process

Clean, test, replace, and verify audio clarity.
01

Audio Signal Diagnostics

We inject test tones at multiple frequencies and measure impedance across each speaker channel. A multi-meter and oscilloscope help identify shorted coils, open circuits, or mechanical rubbing.

02

Cleaning & Grille Restoration

Many "speaker problems" are simply clogged grille mesh. We use ultrasonic cleaning and fine vacuum tools to restore airflow. If the coil is blown, the speaker module is replaced.

03

Component Replacement

Faulty earpiece, bottom speaker, or microphone assemblies are removed and replaced with OEM-grade components. All waterproof seals are restored with fresh adhesive.

04

Full Audio Spectrum Test

We run a 4-point audio certification: frequency response sweep, maximum volume distortion check, microphone sensitivity test, and noise cancellation verification.

Common Questions

Speaker Repair FAQs

Can you fix a crackling speaker without replacement?

Sometimes. Crackling is often caused by debris in the speaker grille or magnetic gap. We can try ultrasonic cleaning first. If the voice coil or diaphragm is physically damaged, the module needs replacement.

My microphone stopped working — is it a software issue?

Microphone failure can be software-related (app permissions, iOS/Android settings) or hardware (corroded flex, blocked mesh, failed MEMS). We check software settings first, then proceed with hardware diagnosis if needed.

Will water damage affect my speakers?

Yes — water exposure is a common cause of speaker failure. Moisture can corrode the voice coil, short the flex connections, or leave mineral deposits that distort sound. We offer combined audio + water damage repair services.
