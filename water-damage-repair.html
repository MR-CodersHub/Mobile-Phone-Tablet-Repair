What We Do

Liquid Damage Restoration

Water is the #1 cause of sudden phone failure. When liquid enters your device, it creates conductive paths that short circuits and causes corrosion that spreads over time. Our restoration process begins the moment your device arrives — we disassemble, clean ultrasonically, treat corrosion chemically, and test each subsystem.

We handle all liquid types: clean water, salt water, soda, coffee, and other corrosive drinks. The sooner we intervene, the higher the success rate. Our lab-grade ultrasonic cleaners and anti-corrosion treatments give your device the best possible chance.

How It Works

Water Damage Recovery Process

Every minute counts. Here is our emergency restoration workflow.
01

Emergency Intake

We advise you to power off immediately and bring your device in. Our team begins triage within minutes — removing the SIM tray, assessing liquid indicator status, and preparing for disassembly.

02

Full Disassembly & Inspection

Every component is removed: screen, battery, logic board, cameras, sensors, buttons. We photograph the corrosion pattern and identify damage on the PCB, connectors, and flex cables.

03

Ultrasonic Cleaning & Chemistry

The logic board and components undergo ultrasonic cleaning with specialized PCBA-safe solutions. Anti-corrosion treatment is applied to neutralize residual electrolytic activity.

04

Reassembly & Subsystem Testing

After drying and inspection under microscope, the device is reassembled. Each subsystem is tested individually: power delivery, display, touch, audio, camera, radios, and sensors. A full device function test follows.

Common Questions

Water Damage FAQs

My phone fell in water. What should I do immediately?

Power it off immediately. Do NOT plug it in to charge. Do NOT put it in rice (rice does not absorb internal moisture and can introduce dust). Wipe the exterior dry and bring it to us as soon as possible for professional disassembly and cleaning.

What is the success rate for water damage repair?

Success depends on how quickly we intervene, the type of liquid, and whether the phone was powered on when wet. With prompt professional cleaning (within 24-48 hours), success rates exceed 70%. Salt water and sugary drinks are more corrosive and require faster action.

Is water damage covered by warranty?

Standard manufacturer warranties do not cover liquid damage. However, our water damage restoration includes a 90-day warranty on the repair work. Some insurance plans may cover water damage — we can provide documentation for your claim.
