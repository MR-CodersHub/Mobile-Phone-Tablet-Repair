What We Do

Charging Port Repair & Replace

A faulty charging port can be caused by pocket lint compaction, corrosion from moisture exposure, or worn pins from repeated use. We begin with a microscopic inspection and deep clean, then replace the entire charging flex assembly if needed — including the port, microphone, and antenna flex in one integrated unit.

Every repair includes amperage draw testing to verify the port is delivering full charging current and passing data without errors.

How It Works

Charging Port Process

Diagnose, clean, replace, and verify.
01

Diagnostic & Inspection

We measure charging current at idle and under load, check for intermittent connections, and inspect the port under magnification for bent pins, corrosion, or debris.

02

Deep Clean or Replace

If the port has debris or light oxidation, we use specialized picks and ultrasonic cleaning. For damaged or worn ports, the entire flex assembly is replaced.

03

Assembly & Sealing

The new charging flex is installed with proper EMI shielding and waterproof gaskets restored. All daughterboard screws are torqued to spec.

04

Power & Data Test

We run a full verification: fast charging amperage draw, data sync with a computer, accessory detection (OTG), and microphone function since these share the same flex cable.

Common Questions

Charging Port FAQs

Will cleaning my charging port fix the issue?

In about 40% of cases, pocket lint compaction is the only problem. We always start with a micro-inspection and deep clean before recommending a full flex replacement. You only pay for what is needed.

Does charging port repair affect wireless charging?

No. The charging port flex cable is separate from the wireless charging coil. Replacing the wired port has no effect on your device's ability to charge wirelessly via Qi or MagSafe.

How do I know if my port needs cleaning vs replacement?

If the cable clicks in loosely or falls out, the port likely needs replacement. If it clicks in firmly but still does not charge, cleaning often resolves the issue. Our free diagnostic determines the exact cause.
