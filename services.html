How long does a typical repair take?
Most common repairs like screen and battery replacements take around 15 to 30 minutes. You can wait at our service center counter, grab a drink, or browse nearby stores while we finish.
Do you offer a warranty on repairs?
Yes! We offer a lifetime warranty on parts and labor for screen and battery replacements. If touch accuracy degrades, simply visit our service center for a free replacement.
Will I lose my data during the repair?
No. We do not access or reset your personal data during screen, port, or battery repairs. However, we advise creating a backup prior to the repair for hardware safety.
Do you use original components?
We use original grade premium components (OEM specifications) which undergo strict quality testing to ensure identical color profile, refresh speed, and touch sensitivity.
